RoStats

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

RoStats is an analytics dashboard for Roblox developers. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Roblox account info. When you sign in with Roblox we receive your Roblox user ID, username, display name and avatar picture. We do not receive your password, email or Robux balance.
  • Games you add. The universe IDs and names of games you add to your dashboard.
  • Files you upload. CSV exports from the Roblox Creator Dashboard that you choose to upload.
  • AI usage. Messages you send to the chatbot, prompts and reference images for the Art Generator, the images it creates, and counts of how much you used these features.

How we use it

Only to run RoStats for you: showing your dashboard, generating charts and AI answers about your own data, keeping your image history, and enforcing plan limits. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

  • Roblox, for sign in and public game information.
  • Google Gemini, which processes your chatbot messages, uploaded data summaries and art prompts to produce answers and images.
  • Vercel (hosting) and Neon (database), which store and serve the data above.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. The Art Generator keeps your 12 most recent images. You can delete everything at any time in Settings with "Delete my data", which removes your account, games, uploaded files and images.

Children

RoStats is a tool for game developers and is not directed at children under 13.
